---
title: The Glade.
summary: I shiver. The sun is like a boat on the river.
image: /images/the-glade.jpg
---

I anticipated it rising  
and worried it wouldn't feel good.  
But when the rays danced across my skin  
The warmth spread as far as it could.  

It now plasters the grass in a glossy green glow  
In fact, all of the field performs a great show  
Laughter, birds chirping, wind through my hair,  
I want to bottle it up nicely and share  

Flowers bloom yellow, orange, and blue  
The shadows before make these colors new  
Pinks, whites, and purples all dance in my eyes  
This is the first time for the sun to rise.  

I giggle, skip, and spin around  
I run my warm fingers on the cool ground  
The clouds paint bright bubbles on the back of my lids  
When I open them I feel like a kid.  

I want more, more, more.  
I can't believe I've never seen this before.  
But a cold wind whispers through my clothes  
And I wonder if my company already knows...  

I shiver.  
The sun is like a boat on a river.  
Away it goes  
Away it flows.  

The warmth will wane!  
The people will leave! 

I can't get it out of my mind  
Will this be the last joy I'll find?  
Only the shadows will stay.  
I worry, I worry, I worry all day.  

The sun trickles onto the hills,  
Away from me, taking the last of its thrills.  
I reach for the last rays above my head,  
And wish that I never got out of bed.
