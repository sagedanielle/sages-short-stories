This winter does not contain as much existential dread as last winter.

My mind becomes more accepting of existence the longer I sit with it. Perhaps I would like to escape, but I have no plans to any longer.

This winter is a rather mild one, which I have mixed feelings about. I would like to feel hard and deep. I would like to feel as raw as I did my first winter, on a clear milkyway night, passing a smoke and a thoughtless chat to my first companion. But that came with a price. It all does.

What a bore, though, to keep doing the same things over and over.
