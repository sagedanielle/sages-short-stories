They always want me to do more.

What looks good on paper? What looks good to employers? What makes you stand out?

No, no, we need something tangible. You can't prove that. Have you been in publications? School clubs? What sport did you lead? Sure you did that, but what else? Okay, what else? Hmm, anything else?

What labs have you worked at with no pay? What have you done for the community for no pay? Yeah, we know this is a capitalist society, but you should give your time for no pay until you have another degree. Yeah, another one. Yeah, we know about that one, you'll need another. Well sure we have money, but that goes to football. At that other place it goes to the C suite.

We've gotten a thousand other applicants, we can't respond to them all.

Any traumatic history? Anything significantly terrible you've overcome?

Yes, we know this is ridiculous. We know you may be better than the person who gets the job. This is the way it works, though. I mean, how the hell else are we supposed to do this?
